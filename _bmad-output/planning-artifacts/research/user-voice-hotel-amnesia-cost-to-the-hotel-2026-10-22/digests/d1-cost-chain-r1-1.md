# D1 cost chain — round 1 (accessed 2026-10-22)
Opened = fetched page read. Snippet = search-result summary only (unverified).

- C1 OPENED. Hinkin & Tracey, Cornell CHR, via Hospitality Net, 22 May 2006. "The average cost of turnover at the front desk was 30 percent of salary, or an average dollar figure of nearly $5,900". Coworkers "lost 20 percent of their productivity for up to 16 days when a colleague left the front desk." Method: CHR web turnover calculator, preliminary; sample not stated. Old, US. class=quantitative. https://www.hospitalitynet.org/news/4027532.html
- C2 OPENED. Edström et al., "Where service recovery meets its paradox", J. Service Theory & Practice 32(7), 2022. Scenario: booked double room unavailable, smaller room offered (SEK 1,500). Compensation to get back to neutral satisfaction ≈ 1,204 SEK, "just over 80% of the original room reservation price" (CI 68-92 %). n=40 + 150, Swedish, scenario-based. https://www.emerald.com/insight/content/doi/10.1108/JSTP-06-2021-0120/full/html
- C3 SNIPPET. Fu et al., IJHM 2015 (PolyU): combined monetary + nonmonetary compensation beats either; nonmonetary better when hotel caused failure. https://research.polyu.edu.hk/en/publications/monetary-or-nonmonetary-compensation-for-service-failure-a-study-/
- C4 SNIPPET (multiple trade write-ups agree: eHotelier, Loyalty360, Lodging). Cornell Hospitality Report "Assessing the Benefits of Rewards Programs" (Stash case): 24 independent hotels, >50,000 guests, 2+ yrs transactions; members returned ~50 % more often; incremental revenue $405-$780 per guest per year, driven by more room nights not rate. Vendor-linked case (Stash), date not confirmed (~2015). https://loyalty360.org/Content-Gallery/Daily-News/Study-Shows-Stash-Hotel-Rewards-Customers-Increase-Spend-by-Nearly
- C5 SNIPPET. Anderson, Cornell CHR 2012: +1 point on 5-pt review scale -> price +11.2 % at same occupancy (Travelocity data); ReviewPro/STR: +1 % reputation -> up to +0.89 % ADR, +1.42 % RevPAR. Old. https://ecommons.cornell.edu/entities/publication/d64bbbba-651a-4e13-a058-af3e2e82f4cf
